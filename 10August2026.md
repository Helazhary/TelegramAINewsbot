# Tech & AI News Briefing — August 10, 2026

The biggest story is that **Jeff Dean's new startup, Discovery Loop**, has landed seed funding from a heavyweight investor syndicate just days after he and three other senior Google researchers walked away from Google to build it — a concrete signal that "AI for automating science itself" is now attracting serious capital, not just headlines.

## 1. Jeff Dean's Discovery Loop lands seed funding from Radical Ventures, Khosla, and Alphabet itself

Days after **Jeff Dean** left Google after 27 years to co-found **Discovery Loop** — a public-benefit corporation aiming to automate machine learning and scientific research — the startup disclosed **seed funding led by Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures**, with participation from Lightspeed, Kleiner Perkins, Doerr Capital, and, notably, **Alphabet itself** taking a stake. Co-founders **Sanjay Ghemawat**, **Oriol Vinyals**, and **Quoc Le** joined Dean from Google. The team's pitch: AI systems that propose experiments, run them, learn from results, and iterate recursively — starting with automating ML research before moving to engineering, medicine, materials, and clean energy.

- **Why this matters for you (developer/entrepreneur):** A16z-caliber investors backing "self-driving R&D" just weeks after a major lab reshuffle shows where elite AI talent believes the next big value pool is — automating the scientific/engineering discovery loop itself, not another chat interface.
- **Business/project idea:** Build narrow, defensible tooling in the discovery-automation stack before it gets crowded — e.g., an experiment-tracking/provenance layer purpose-built for AI-run research pipelines, or domain-specific "lab-in-the-loop" APIs (chemistry, materials, biology) that a startup like Discovery Loop would want to plug into rather than rebuild.
- **Sources:** [Radical Ventures — Our Investment in Discovery Loop](https://radical.vc/our-investment-in-discovery-loop/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/), [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/)

## 2. US Department of Energy launches Genesis Open Models Initiative for science

The **Department of Energy**, under its executive-order-backed **Genesis Mission**, launched an initiative to co-fund and co-develop a new class of **open-weight foundation models for scientific research** — targeting materials discovery, energy systems, earth modeling, fusion, biology, and high-energy physics. The first model, **Genesis-Science-1**, is being built in partnership with **Arcee.ai**. Applications for the first contribution window (pretraining) close **August 14, 2026**; fine-tuning applications close August 25.

- **Why this matters for you (developer/entrepreneur):** This is a rare case of a US government agency directly co-developing and funding open-weight AI models with industry — a concrete, dated opportunity if you have ML or scientific-domain expertise, with an application deadline just days away.
- **Business/project idea:** If you have domain expertise in a covered scientific area, apply to the fine-tuning contribution window (deadline Aug 25) to co-develop a specialized Genesis model — or build downstream tooling (eval harnesses, fine-tuning pipelines, domain benchmarks) aimed at teams participating in the initiative.
- **Sources:** [Department of Energy (official)](https://www.energy.gov/undersecretaryforscience/articles/us-department-energy-launches-genesis-open-models-initiative), [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49216946)

## 3. Uber commits $10B to deploy 120,000 robotaxis across 15 cities

CEO **Dara Khosrowshahi** announced Uber plans to invest **over $10 billion over the coming years** to deploy roughly **120,000 autonomous vehicles** across **15+ cities**, funded from a position of financial strength — trailing-12-month free cash flow of roughly **$10.1 billion**. Rather than building self-driving tech in-house, Uber's strategy leans on partnerships (it's already working with Wayve, Waymo, and others) to scale fastest, positioning itself as the dispatch/demand layer across multiple AV suppliers rather than a single vertically integrated stack.

- **Why this matters for you (developer/entrepreneur):** Uber betting big on being the neutral aggregation layer across AV suppliers — rather than picking one horse — validates a platform strategy worth studying for any market where multiple competing AI/hardware providers are converging (foundation models, robotics, agents).
- **Business/project idea:** Build supporting infrastructure for the multi-operator AV transition Uber is now committing capital to — e.g., a fleet-health/telemetry dashboard that normalizes data across different AV stacks (Wayve, Waymo, others) for city regulators or fleet operators who need vendor-agnostic oversight.

## 4. Kimi K3 goes generally available in GitHub Copilot — a 2.8T-parameter open-weight frontier model

Moonshot AI's **Kimi K3**, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model with native multimodal input, a **1-million-token context window**, and frontier-level agentic coding performance, is now **generally available in GitHub Copilot** (hosted via Fireworks AI), priced at **$3/1M input, $15/1M output, $0.30/1M cached input tokens**. It follows Kimi K2.7's Copilot debut a month earlier as GitHub's first open-weight model offering. Enterprise/Business admins must explicitly enable it via a Copilot policy setting before developers can select it.

- **Why this matters for you (developer/entrepreneur):** A frontier-capable open-weight coding model landing natively inside the world's most-used AI coding tool — at proprietary-model pricing, not a discount — signals GitHub is now treating open-weight Chinese labs as first-class citizens alongside OpenAI/Anthropic/Google in its model marketplace.
- **Business/project idea:** If you're building Copilot extensions or internal dev-tooling, benchmark Kimi K3 against your default model for long-context, agentic, multi-file coding tasks — its 1M-token window may let you skip retrieval/chunking infrastructure you'd otherwise need to build for large-repo reasoning.
- **Sources:** [GitHub Changelog (official)](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-06-kimi-k3-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/), [MoonshotAI/Kimi-K3 (Hugging Face/GitHub)](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Kimi-K3), [Simon Willison](https://simonw.substack.com/p/kimi-k3-and-what-we-can-still-learn)

## 5. Amazon's Q2 raises the bar on AI infrastructure spend: AWS growth hits an 18-quarter high

Amazon posted **Q2 2026 revenue of $200.6B** (+20% YoY), with **AWS growth accelerating to 36.7%** — its fastest pace in 18 quarters — and operating margin reaching 39%. Amazon's **AI and custom-chip businesses each now exceed a $25B annual run rate**, growing triple digits, with **Anthropic and OpenAI** among the AI labs making multi-year, multi-gigawatt commitments to Amazon's **Trainium** chips. Management raised full-year **2026 capex guidance to $220B**, citing AI demand it expects to outstrip supply into 2027–2028.

- **Why this matters for you (developer/entrepreneur):** Two frontier labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) locking in multi-gigawatt Trainium commitments alongside Nvidia usage confirms that compute diversification across chip vendors is now standard practice at the top of the market — a pattern worth mirroring in your own infrastructure choices to avoid single-vendor pricing/capacity risk.
- **Business/project idea:** With Amazon signaling AI compute will stay supply-constrained through 2027-2028, build or resell tooling that helps smaller teams get more out of scarce/expensive GPU time — e.g., inference-cost optimization, spot-capacity brokering across clouds, or workload-scheduling software that arbitrages price/availability across AWS, GCP, and Azure.
